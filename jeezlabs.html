jeezlabs is a two-person studio that makes software, sketches, and occasional physical objects — mostly on weekends, mostly for the fun of it.

we're interested in tools that feel like toys, interfaces that behave like instruments, and the kind of small weird things that would never survive a product roadmap.

we ship when it's ready. sometimes that's a tuesday. sometimes it's never.
